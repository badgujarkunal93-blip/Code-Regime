Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PivotVault. Have another question? Get in touch with our team.

General

PivotVault is an AI-powered Startup Intelligence Platform that helps founders, investors, and startup teams learn from historical startup successes and failures through AI-powered insights, structured data, and interactive analysis.

ChatGPT provides general responses. PivotVault combines structured startup data, AI analysis, historical case studies, and live research to deliver startup-specific intelligence and actionable recommendations.

PivotVault aggregates publicly available startup information from trusted sources such as company announcements, founder interviews, news articles, startup databases, public reports, and AI-powered web research.

PivotVault is designed for founders, entrepreneurs, investors, startup incubators, accelerators, researchers, and students who want to make informed startup decisions.

Platform

Describe your startup idea, and PivotVault analyzes similar companies, market trends, competition, historical failures, and potential risks to generate actionable recommendations.

Yes. PivotVault allows side-by-side comparisons of startups based on funding, business models, founders, timelines, competitors, and key lessons.

The Knowledge Graph visualizes relationships between startups, founders, investors, industries, competitors, acquisitions, and historical events to uncover patterns that are difficult to see in traditional databases.

Yes. Users can bookmark startups, save collections, and revisit AI-generated insights for future reference.

AI & Security

PivotVault combines structured startup data with AI and publicly available sources to generate contextual insights. AI recommendations are designed to support decision-making, not replace human judgment.

Yes. User accounts, uploaded documents, and personal information are securely managed using modern authentication and encryption practices.

Yes. PivotVault analyzes startup ideas and pitch decks to provide AI-powered feedback, identify potential risks, and suggest improvements.
